---
name: YTD management month
description: Distinguish the YTD KPI cutoff from the dashboard's default last-closed-month query window
---

The company-wide management month is an administrator-selected persisted setting, independent of the real calendar month. Current-month revenue, operating profit, charts, tables, and their period badges must use it.

**Why:** Management reporting can intentionally operate on a month different from both the real calendar month and the previous closed month. Deriving it from either made the dashboard change without an administrator's decision.

**How to apply:** Read the shared setting for default month windows and current-month aggregation. Explicit user date ranges still take precedence; full-year cards remain January through December. Only administrators may update the shared value.
